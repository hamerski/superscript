Physics Computational Literacy is a framework that breaks downs the key components of literacy needed to effectively use computing in physics. Definitions from [Nearhood and Hamerski, 2025](https://www.zotero.org/groups/6316980/superscript/items/X869F3DZ):
- Material (MCL): How people practically use computing, such as through the act of coding.
- Cognitive (CCL): How people understand the computing they're doing, and how they use it to understand physics.
- Social (SCL) How people work together with computing, and communicate what they’re doing and understanding.
