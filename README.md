# superSCRIPT

This repo is a wiki for members of the superSCRIPT lab to access and contribute useful info. We are an education research lab led by [Patti Hamerski](https://hamerski.github.io/) in the [Department of Physics](https://physics.oregonstate.edu/) at Oregon State University.

In this repo, you will (eventually) be able to find:
- Lab norms and expectations for members
- Breakdowns of helpful research frameworks
- Collaborative research products (e.g., our lab bibfile)
- Guides for using research software (e.g., MAXQDA)
- Information on relevant conferences and publication venues
- Resources for navigating departmental and university processes

This wiki was built through the ongoing effort of superSCRIPT lab members, including:
